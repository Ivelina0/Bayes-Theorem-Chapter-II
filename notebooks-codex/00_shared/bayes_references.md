# Bayesian Computation References

## MCMC

- Hastings, W. K. (1970). "Monte Carlo Sampling Methods Using Markov Chains and Their Applications."
- Robert, C. P., and Casella, G. (2004). *Monte Carlo Statistical Methods*.

- Gordon, N. J., Salmond, D. J., and Smith, A. F. M. (1993). "Novel Approach to Nonlinear/Non-Gaussian Bayesian State Estimation."
